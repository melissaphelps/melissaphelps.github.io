Selected Work

Production Control & Communication System

A connected operational system that turns scattered project information into clear actions, accountable follow-up, and focused decision support.

The system was designed to reduce repeated manual checking, strengthen communication, improve follow-up, and make operational information easier to act on across the organization.

TW watches • DANB decides • CJC connects • FCS bridges • TEAS escalates

  • Target Watch (TW) monitors target dates, schedule movement, overdue activity, follow-up needs, and stalled work.
  • Daily Action & Notes Builder (DANB) converts project information into a focused daily action, documentation, and follow-up workflow.
  • Communication Junction Center (CJC) serves as the administrative intake and central connection point for operational information and communication.
  • Field Communication System (FCS) acts as a two-way bridge connecting the office with both the field team and clients.
  • Team Escalation & Alert System (TEAS) identifies unresolved issues and directs needed attention to the appropriate team members.

Human-reviewed by design: AI may assist with organizing information and drafting communication, but source validation, exceptions, approval, and final decisions remain with people.
